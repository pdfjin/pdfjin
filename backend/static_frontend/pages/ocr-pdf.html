Convert Scanned PDFs and Images to Searchable Text with OCR

Transform flat, scanned PDF documents and image files into fully searchable, selectable, and editable PDFs with PDFjin's advanced free online OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool. If you have non-searchable document scans, receipts, or page photos where you cannot copy text, our intelligent OCR engine reads page characters, matching them to digital fonts, and overlays selectable invisible text layers onto your pages with stunning accuracy. Ideal for researchers, legal clerks, and student archiving, our online OCR utility operates without login requirements or software installs. The process supports multiple languages and handles files up to 50MB quickly. PDFjin operates on secure cloud nodes to protect your records, automatically purging all data from our servers post-OCR to guarantee absolute confidentiality.
