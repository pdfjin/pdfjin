Frequently Asked Questions About Excel To Pdf

Conversion Accuracy

Will all my spreadsheet tabs be converted?

Yes, by default, all active worksheets within your Excel file (.xlsx or .xls) will be sequentially converted into pages in the resulting PDF document.

How does it handle very wide spreadsheets?

Our converter automatically scales wide spreadsheets to fit horizontally on a standard page size (like A4 or Letter) to ensure columns aren't abruptly cut off.

Are formulas and calculations preserved?

The PDF captures the final calculated values exactly as they appeared in Excel. The underlying formulas themselves are not interactive in the static PDF.

Formatting Preservation

Will my cell colors and borders be maintained?

Absolutely. All visual formatting, including background fill colors, custom borders, bold text, and conditional formatting results are perfectly replicated in the PDF.

Does the converter retain Excel charts and graphs?

Yes, any embedded charts, graphs, or pivot tables visible on the worksheet will be rendered accurately as high-quality vector graphics in the PDF.

Can I set the page orientation to landscape?

The converter respects the page layout settings defined in your original Excel file. If you set the worksheet to Landscape in Excel, it will be Landscape in the PDF.
