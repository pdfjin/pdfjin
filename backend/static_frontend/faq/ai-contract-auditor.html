Frequently Asked Questions About Ai Contract Auditor

Clause Detection & Risk Analysis

What specific types of clauses does the AI look for?

The auditor is trained to flag potentially problematic clauses such as auto-renewals, hidden fees, aggressive non-competes, one-sided termination rights, and unusual liability limitations.

Can it replace a human lawyer for contract review?

No. Our AI provides a fast, preliminary risk assessment to highlight areas of concern, but it does not constitute formal legal advice. You should always consult a qualified attorney for final review.

Does the tool work with non-English legal contracts?

Currently, our AI models are heavily optimized for English language legal terminology and common law frameworks. Results in other languages may be significantly less accurate.

Output & Reporting

How are the risks presented in the final report?

Risks are categorized by severity (High, Medium, Low) and include a plain-English explanation of why the clause is flagged, along with the direct quote from your document.

Will the auditor suggest alternative wording for bad clauses?

While it highlights the risks and explains the potential negative impact, it does not currently rewrite or generate alternative legal phrasing for you.

Can I export the audit report to share with my legal team?

Yes, you can easily export the summary dashboard as a PDF report or print it directly from the browser to share with your colleagues or legal counsel.
