Frequently Asked Questions About Watermark Pdf

Customization Options

Can I use an image (like my company logo) as a watermark?

Yes, you can upload a PNG or JPG image, adjust its opacity so it doesn't obscure the text, and stamp it across the pages of your PDF.

Is it possible to add text watermarks like 'CONFIDENTIAL'?

Absolutely. You can type custom text, choose the font, color, and size, and rotate it diagonally to create a prominent, professional text watermark.

Can I choose which pages get the watermark?

Currently, the watermark is applied uniformly across all pages of the document to ensure consistent branding or security marking.

Formatting & Security

Does the watermark sit in front of or behind the text?

You have the option to place the watermark over the page content (best for security) or behind the page content (best for subtle branding like letterheads).

Can someone easily remove the watermark?

While we flatten the watermark into the document as best as possible, advanced PDF editors can sometimes isolate objects. For maximum security, we recommend flattening the PDF afterward.

Will adding a watermark significantly increase the file size?

Adding a text watermark has almost zero impact on file size. Adding a high-resolution image logo may slightly increase the size, but it is generally negligible.
