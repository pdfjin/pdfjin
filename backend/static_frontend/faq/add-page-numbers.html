Frequently Asked Questions About Add Page Numbers

Numbering Options & Formatting

Can I choose where the page numbers appear?

Yes, you can select from multiple predefined positions including top-left, top-center, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-center, and bottom-right to perfectly align with your document layout.

Is it possible to start numbering from a specific page?

While the default setting numbers all pages sequentially, advanced options allow you to skip the cover page or begin the numbering index from any specific page number.

Will the page numbers overwrite existing text?

Our engine intelligently places the numbers in the standard margin areas. We recommend ensuring your PDF has adequate margins to prevent overlapping with existing content.

Customization & Style

Can I change the font style and size of the numbers?

Currently, we use a standard, professional sans-serif font optimized for readability across all document types and printing formats.

Does this tool support Bates numbering for legal documents?

This tool is designed for standard sequential page numbering. For complex Bates stamping with prefixes and suffixes, please check our specialized legal tools.

What happens if I combine multiple PDFs before numbering?

It's highly recommended to use our Merge PDF tool first to combine your files into a single document, and then apply page numbers so the sequence is continuous.
