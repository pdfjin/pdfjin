Frequently Asked Questions About Merge Pdf

Merging Capabilities

How do I control the order of the merged documents?

After uploading your files, you will see a visual interface where you can drag and drop the document thumbnails into your exact desired sequence before merging.

Can I merge PDFs that have different page sizes?

Yes, the tool seamlessly combines documents with varying dimensions (e.g., a mix of A4, Letter, and Landscape pages) into one file without cropping or distorting them.

Is there a limit to how many files I can combine at once?

You can merge dozens of files simultaneously in a single session, making it ideal for compiling large end-of-month reports or legal portfolios.

File Integrity

Will merging PDFs degrade their quality?

Not at all. The merge process is completely lossless. It concatenates the structural data of the PDFs without re-rendering or compressing text and images.

What happens to existing bookmarks and hyperlinks?

Standard internal hyperlinks and text formatting remain intact. However, complex navigational bookmarks from individual files may be simplified in the combined document.

Can I merge password-protected files?

You must remove the encryption from protected files using our Unlock PDF tool before they can be merged with other documents.
