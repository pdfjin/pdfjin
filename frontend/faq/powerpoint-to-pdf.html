Frequently Asked Questions About Powerpoint To Pdf

Conversion Reliability

Will my custom fonts be preserved in the PDF?

Yes, all text is embedded directly into the PDF, meaning your exact typography, font sizes, and layout will look identical regardless of what device the PDF is viewed on.

What happens to my slide transitions and animations?

PDF is a static document format. Slide transitions, audio clips, videos, and entrance animations will not function in the resulting PDF file. It will show the final state of each slide.

Does the conversion keep my hidden slides?

By default, the converter only processes the visible slides in your presentation. Hidden slides are ignored to ensure the PDF reflects your actual presentation flow.

Formatting & Output

Will speaker notes be included in the PDF?

This tool converts the primary slide view. Standard conversion does not append the speaker notes to the bottom of the PDF pages.

Is the resulting PDF suitable for high-quality printing?

Absolutely. The text remains as crisp vector data and images are kept at a high resolution, making the PDF perfect for printing handouts or professional portfolios.

Can I convert older .ppt files or only .pptx?

Our engine supports both the modern XML-based .pptx format as well as legacy .ppt files from older versions of Microsoft Office.
