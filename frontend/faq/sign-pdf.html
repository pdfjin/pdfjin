Frequently Asked Questions About Sign Pdf

Signature Creation

How can I add my signature to the document?

You have three options: you can draw your signature using your mouse or touchscreen, type your name using cursive fonts, or upload a PNG image of your actual signature.

Is it easy to sign documents on a mobile phone?

Yes, this tool is highly optimized for mobile devices. Using your finger on a smartphone touchscreen is actually the most natural way to draw a smooth, accurate signature.

Can I resize and move the signature once placed?

Absolutely. After dropping your signature onto the page, you can drag it to the exact signature line and use the corner handles to scale it perfectly to fit the space.

Legality & Security

Are these digital signatures legally binding?

In most countries (including the US and EU), electronic signatures are legally binding for standard contracts and agreements under the ESIGN Act and eIDAS, but you should verify local laws for highly sensitive legal instruments.

Does this tool use cryptographic certificate signing?

This tool provides visual Electronic Signatures (e-signatures), which are sufficient for 99% of business use cases. It does not append cryptographic Digital Certificates (PKI).

Can I add a date and text fields along with my signature?

Yes, the signing interface includes a text tool that allows you to easily type the current date, your printed name, or your job title next to your signature.
