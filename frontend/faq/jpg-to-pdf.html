Frequently Asked Questions About Jpg To Pdf

Image Handling & Layout

Can I convert multiple JPGs into a single PDF document?

Yes, you can upload dozens of JPG images at once, reorder them via drag-and-drop, and combine them all into one continuous PDF portfolio.

Will the tool stretch or distort my images?

No, images are scaled proportionally to fit the page without altering their aspect ratio. You can choose whether to add margins or have the image fill the page.

Does it reduce the quality of my photographs?

The converter maintains the original resolution and color depth of your JPGs. We do not apply heavy compression during conversion unless you specifically request it.

Format Support

Does this tool only accept JPG files?

While optimized for JPG/JPEG, the tool also seamlessly accepts other common image formats like PNG, BMP, and GIF, converting them all into a unified PDF.

Can I choose the page size (e.g., A4 or Letter)?

Yes, before converting, you can select standard document sizes like A4, US Letter, or configure the PDF page to match the exact dimensions of the image.

Is this tool useful for scanning documents with a phone camera?

Perfectly. You can take photos of physical documents with your phone, upload the JPGs here, and instantly generate a professional multi-page PDF.
