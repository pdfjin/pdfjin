Frequently Asked Questions About Ai Semantic Extract

Concept-Based Search

How is this different from regular text search?

Standard search requires exact keyword matches (e.g., 'car'). Semantic extraction understands meaning, so searching for 'vehicle' will successfully extract paragraphs mentioning 'cars', 'trucks', and 'automobiles'.

Can I use it to find specific arguments or sentiments?

Absolutely. You can prompt the AI to 'extract all paragraphs expressing a negative opinion about the product' and it will identify them based on context and tone.

Does it highlight the text in the original PDF?

The tool extracts the relevant text blocks and presents them to you in a clean, compiled list rather than highlighting and modifying the original PDF file.

Processing Details

How much context is extracted around the semantic match?

The AI typically extracts the complete paragraph surrounding the matching concept to ensure you have full context, rather than just returning a fragmented sentence.

Can I extract data based on a temporal concept?

Yes, you can ask for 'events that happened before 2020' or 'future projections', and the semantic engine will understand the timeline context.

Is semantic extraction faster than reading the document?

Drastically. It can semantically parse and extract concepts from a 50-page document in seconds, a task that would take a human reader over an hour to accomplish manually.
