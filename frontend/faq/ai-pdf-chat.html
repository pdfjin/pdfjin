Frequently Asked Questions About Ai Pdf Chat

Interactive Document Querying

Can I ask questions about specific pages or sections?

Yes! You can ask the AI to summarize chapter 3, find specific statistics, or explain a complex paragraph in simple terms, and it will draw answers directly from the document's text.

Does the AI hallucinate or invent information not in the PDF?

The model is strictly constrained to use only the context provided within your uploaded PDF, drastically minimizing hallucinations. If the answer isn't in the document, it will tell you.

How many questions can I ask in a single session?

You can have a continuous, multi-turn conversation. Follow-up questions are supported, allowing you to drill down into complex topics mentioned in previous answers.

Technical Capabilities

Does the chat tool understand tables and financial data?

Yes, the AI can interpret structured data within tables, allowing you to ask questions about revenue figures, comparisons, or specific data points listed in the document.

Can it translate parts of the PDF for me?

Absolutely. You can ask the AI to translate specific paragraphs or summarize a foreign-language document into your native language right in the chat window.

Is the chat history saved for my next visit?

For your privacy and security, all chat histories and uploaded documents are completely cleared from our systems as soon as you close the session or navigate away.
