Merging multiple PDF files on Mac - A step-by-step guide

By PDFjin Content Team • May 29, 2026 • 6 min read
Share:

Master the Art - How to Merge Multiple PDF Files on Mac Effortlessly

Clutter is the ultimate enemy of productivity. Imagine preparing for an important business presentation or submitting a university application. You have your cover letter, resume, portfolios, and reference letters scattered across your desktop in separate documents. Sending five different attachments to a client or recruiter looks unprofessional and invites chaos.

Combining these documents into a single, cohesive file makes your work look polished. macOS offers several incredibly powerful built-in tools to help you manage this task. In this comprehensive guide, we will explore the best methods to merge multiple PDF files on Mac. We will cover native options like Preview and Finder Quick Actions, alongside highly efficient online alternatives.

Method 1 - Merging PDFs Using macOS Preview

Apple Preview is the default application for viewing images and PDFs on macOS. While it seems like a simple viewer, Preview hides some robust editing features. You can use it to combine entire files or insert specific pages from one document into another.

To merge two entire PDF files, follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Open your first PDF file in the Preview application.
  • Step 2: Go to the top menu bar and select View, then click Thumbnails. This action opens a sidebar displaying thumbnail images of all pages in your document.
  • Step 3: Select the thumbnail of the page where you want to insert the other document. Usually, this is the last page.
  • Step 4: Click on Edit in the menu bar, hover over Insert, and choose Page from File.
  • Step 5: Browse your Mac's storage, select the second PDF file you wish to add, and click Open.
  • Step 6: Save your newly combined document by selecting File and then Export as PDF.

This method works beautifully when you want to combine entire documents quickly. Preview allows you to visual inspect your changes before saving, ensuring your pages look correct.

How to Merge Specific Pages from Different PDFs

Sometimes you do not need to merge entire documents. You might only need page three of an invoice and page five of a project report. Preview handles this selective merging with ease using drag-and-drop actions.

First, open both PDF documents in separate Preview windows. Make sure you enable the Thumbnail view in both windows by navigating to View and selecting Thumbnails. Arrange the two windows side-by-side on your desktop screen.

Next, click and hold the thumbnail of the specific page you want to copy. Drag it across the screen and drop it into the thumbnail sidebar of the destination PDF. You can place the new page anywhere in the document. You can drop it at the beginning, middle, or end. Once you achieve the perfect order, save your destination document to secure your edits.

Method 2 - Using Finder Quick Actions (The Fastest Way)

If you are in a rush and need to combine files instantly, Apple built a shortcut directly into the Finder app. This method requires no applications to open. It is the absolute fastest way to merge files on a Mac.

To use Finder Quick Actions, locate the files you want to combine in your Finder window. Highlight all of them by holding down the Command key while clicking each file. Make sure you select them in the exact order you want them to appear in the final document.

Once you select your files, right-click on the highlighted group to open the context menu. Scroll down and hover over Quick Actions. Click on Create PDF. macOS will immediately generate a new, combined PDF file in the same folder. It preserves your original files and creates a newly merged file within seconds.

Why Built-In Mac Tools Might Fall Short

While Preview and Finder Quick Actions are convenient, they are not always perfect. Preview can struggle with massive file sizes, sometimes lagging or crashing during the saving process. It also lacks advanced features such as automatic page layout adjustments, compression, or smart text recognition.

Furthermore, managing page order in Preview can feel clunky if you are dealing with dozens of pages. If you run into these limitations, a web-based solution can save your sanity. You can seamlessly merge PDF files online using a modern browser. Web tools process heavy files on remote cloud servers, keeping your Mac running fast and cool.

How to Reorganize Pages After Merging

A common issue when combining documents is incorrect page orientation or sequence. A document scanned upside down or a misplaced page ruins your presentation. If your pages end up in the wrong order, you can easily reorder PDF pages in your browser before exporting your final copy.

If you accidentally combine too many pages or include sensitive information, don't worry. You can also extract or split PDF pages with a single click. This flexibility ensures your final file contains only the necessary information, keeping your communication precise and professional.

Pro Tips for Managing PDFs on Mac

To keep your digital workflow smooth, follow these simple organizational tips:

  • Name your files strategically: Before merging, name your files with prefixes like "01_Cover", "02_Resume", and "03_Portfolio". This makes multi-file selection in Finder much easier.
  • Keep backups of original files: Always keep your separate original files until you are absolutely certain your merged file is correct and complete.
  • Reduce file sizes: Merging many high-resolution documents can result in a massive file size. Remember to compress your combined document before emailing it.

Elevate Your PDF Workflow Today

Now you know how to conquer document clutter on your Mac. Whether you choose the built-in Preview app, utilize Finder Quick Actions, or look for robust web alternatives, you have the power to create clean, organized files in seconds.
