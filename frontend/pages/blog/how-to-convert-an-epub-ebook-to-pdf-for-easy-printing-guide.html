How to convert an EPUB ebook to PDF for easy printing

By PDFjin Content Team • Jul 30, 2026 • 6 min read
Share:

Converting EPUB to PDF for Print Readiness

EPUB files, while excellent for flexible digital reading, are fundamentally reflowable documents. This characteristic, which allows content to adapt to various screen sizes and user-defined font preferences, makes them unsuitable for consistent, fixed-layout printing. To achieve reliable print output, converting your EPUB ebook to a static PDF format is essential. PDFs maintain precise page layouts, ensuring your printed document looks exactly as intended.

Why EPUB Isn't Print Ready

The core difference lies in their design philosophy. EPUB (Electronic Publication) is structured to adapt, meaning page breaks, text flow, and image placement can shift based on the viewing device or reader settings. This dynamic nature provides a poor foundation for print, where exact positioning and consistent page counts are critical.

Conversely, PDF (Portable Document Format) is designed for fixed-layout presentation. It encapsulates all necessary fonts, images, and layout information directly within the file, guaranteeing that the document's appearance remains identical across different software, hardware, and print jobs. For any document destined for a physical printer, PDF is the industry standard.

Converting EPUB to PDF Using Calibre

Calibre is a powerful, open-source ebook management application that provides robust conversion capabilities, making it the preferred tool for converting EPUBs to print-ready PDFs. It offers extensive control over the output format and layout.

Step 1 Install Calibre

  • Download Calibre from its official website.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to install the application on your Windows, macOS, or Linux system.

Step 2 Add Your EPUB File

  • Launch Calibre.
  • Click the "Add books" button and navigate to the location of your EPUB file.
  • Select your EPUB file to import it into your Calibre library.

Step 3 Configure Conversion Settings

  • In your Calibre library, select the book you wish to convert.
  • Click the "Convert books" button in the toolbar.
  • In the "Convert" dialog, ensure "Output format" in the top right corner is set to PDF.
  • Navigate to the "Page Setup" section. Here, you can configure the "Output profile" (e.g., Tablet, Default) and crucially, set page margins. Typically, 0.5 inches for all margins (top, bottom, left, right) provides a good starting point for print.
  • Under "PDF Output," you can further refine settings like paper size (Letter, A4), font embedding, and add headers or footers if needed. Ensure "Detect and convert links to PDF bookmarks" is enabled in "Table of Contents" for a navigable PDF.

Step 4 Initiate Conversion and Save

  • After carefully reviewing all conversion settings to match your printing requirements, click "OK" to begin the process.
  • Once Calibre completes the conversion, right-click on the book in your library.
  • Select "Open containing folder" to directly access your newly created PDF file, ready for printing.

Online EPUB to PDF Converters

For quick, infrequent conversions without software installation, various online tools can convert EPUB to PDF. These services often provide a straightforward upload-and-download process.

  • Search online for "EPUB to PDF converter."
  • Upload your EPUB file, select PDF as the desired output format, and then download the converted PDF.
  • Be mindful of potential file size limitations and data privacy when using free online services, especially with sensitive content.

Post Conversion Tips for Printing

After successfully converting your EPUB to PDF, a few additional steps can optimize the document for the best printing experience.

Check Layout and Margins

Open the converted PDF in a viewer and quickly scroll through every page. Verify that text flows correctly, images are appropriately placed, and margins are sufficient. If any issues are found, consider re-converting with minor adjustments to your Calibre settings.

Add Page Numbers

For any document beyond a few pages, clear page numbering is essential for reference and readability. If your conversion tool did not automatically include them, you can easily add page numbers to your PDF using various online or desktop PDF editing tools.

Optimize File Size

Ebooks, especially those with many images, can result in large PDF files after conversion. Large PDFs can be slow to print and cumbersome to share. If your PDF is significantly large, use a dedicated tool to compress the PDF file to a more manageable size without compromising print quality.
