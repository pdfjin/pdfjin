How to combine Word Excel and Images into one PDF binder

By PDFjin Content Team • Jul 26, 2026 • 6 min read
Share:

Creating a Unified PDF Binder from Mixed Document Types

To combine Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and images into a single PDF binder, the core process involves two main steps: first, converting each source file into its own PDF format, and then merging these individual PDFs into one cohesive document. This approach ensures content integrity and consistent presentation across all diverse file types.

Converting Word Documents to PDF

Microsoft Word provides robust native options for PDF conversion, preserving fonts, layouts, and formatting accurately. This is the most straightforward method for Word files.

Method 1 Save As PDF in Word

  • Open your Word document.
  • Go to 'File' > 'Save As'.
  • Choose a destination for your file.
  • In the 'Save as type' dropdown menu, select 'PDF'.
  • Click 'Save'.

Method 2 Print to PDF

Alternatively, you can use the built-in virtual printer functionality, which is useful for ensuring the PDF output matches the print preview exactly.

  • Open your Word document.
  • Go to 'File' > 'Print'.
  • From the 'Printer' dropdown, select 'Microsoft Print to PDF' (or an equivalent virtual PDF printer like Adobe PDF).
  • Click 'Print' and specify the save location and filename for your PDF.

Converting Excel Spreadsheets to PDF

Converting Excel sheets requires careful attention to print areas and scaling to ensure all data is captured legibly on the PDF pages. Unlike Word, Excel's dynamic layout can lead to pagination issues if not managed correctly.

Defining Print Area and Scaling in Excel

Before converting, always define your print area and adjust scaling within Excel to prevent truncated data or excessive blank pages.

  • Open your Excel workbook.
  • Select the cells or sheets you wish to include in the PDF.
  • Go to 'Page Layout' tab > 'Print Area' > 'Set Print Area'.
  • Under 'Page Setup' (small arrow in the bottom right of the 'Page Setup' group), adjust 'Scaling'. Options like 'Fit Sheet on One Page' or 'Fit All Columns on One Page' are often best for single-sheet outputs.
  • Go to 'File' > 'Print'.
  • Select 'Microsoft Print to PDF' as your printer.
  • Review the print preview carefully. If necessary, adjust page breaks or scaling further.
  • Click 'Print' and save your Excel data as a PDF.

Common Excel to PDF Issues and Solutions

Understanding these common pitfalls helps in creating a clean PDF from your spreadsheets.

Issue Root Cause Solution
Data cut off Print area too small, incorrect scaling Set 'Fit All Columns' or 'Fit Sheet on One Page' in Page Setup. Adjust margins.
Blank pages Hidden columns/rows with data, defined print area includes empty cells Clear print area, re-define accurately. Remove unnecessary columns/rows.
Text too small Too much content forced onto one page Allow more pages (e.g., 'Fit All Columns' without 'Fit All Rows'), or manually adjust scale percentage.

Converting Images to PDF

Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, etc.) can be easily converted to PDF, either individually or in batches. This is essential for incorporating visual elements into your combined document.

Method 1 Using Windows Photos or Preview on Mac

  • Windows: Open the image in the Photos app. Click the printer icon (or Ctrl+P). Select 'Microsoft Print to PDF' as the printer. Adjust layout (e.g., 'Full Page Photo') and click 'Print'.
  • Mac: Open the image in Preview. Go to 'File' > 'Print'. In the print dialog, select 'PDF' > 'Save as PDF'.

Method 2 Using Online Tools

For converting multiple images into a single PDF, or for more advanced options, online tools offer a convenient solution. You can upload multiple image files and they will be combined into one PDF.

  • Navigate to an online JPG to PDF converter (many are available, including PDFjin).
  • Upload your image files.
  • Arrange them in the desired order.
  • Initiate the conversion and download your combined image PDF.

Merging All PDFs into a Single Binder

Once all your Word, Excel, and image files have been converted into individual PDFs, the final step is to combine them into one seamless document. This requires a PDF merging tool.

Using a PDF Merging Tool

Online PDF merging tools offer a simple drag-and-drop interface, making the process quick and intuitive.

  • Open your web browser and navigate to a PDF merging tool, such as PDFjin's Merge Pdf.
  • Upload all the individual PDF files you created (from Word, Excel, and images).
  • Drag and drop the files to arrange them in the exact order you want them to appear in the final binder.
  • Confirm the order and initiate the merge process.
  • Download your new, unified PDF binder.

Optimizing Your PDF Binder

After combining various document types, your final PDF binder might be quite large, especially if it contains many images or detailed spreadsheets. Consider optimizing its size for easier sharing.

If your combined PDF is too large, you can easily compress the PDF file online before distributing it. This helps reduce file size without significant loss of quality, making it more manageable for email or web uploads.
