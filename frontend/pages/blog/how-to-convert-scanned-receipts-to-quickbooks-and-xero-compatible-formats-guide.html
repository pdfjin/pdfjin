How to convert scanned receipts to QuickBooks and Xero compatible formats

By PDFjin Content Team • Oct 01, 2026 • 6 min read
Share:

Bridging the Gap Scanned Receipts to Accounting Software

Scanned receipts, often image-based PDFs, lack the structured data required by modern accounting platforms like QuickBooks and Xero. The core challenge is transforming these static images into searchable, extractable information that your accounting software can process.

To achieve compatibility, you'll need to employ Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to convert image-only scans into searchable PDFs. Following this, data extraction into a structured format such as CSV or Excel becomes feasible, enabling seamless import.

Understanding QuickBooks and Xero Requirements

While both platforms support attaching document images for record-keeping, direct data import relies on specific file types and formats. An image alone won't populate your ledger; you need text and structured data.

QuickBooks Import Specifications

  • Receipt Attachments PDF, JPG, PNG. Searchable PDFs are highly recommended for future reference and data extraction.
  • Data Import CSV or Excel files with specific column headers (e.g., Date, Vendor, Amount, Account).

Xero Import Specifications

  • Receipt Attachments PDF, JPG, PNG. Searchable PDFs are preferred for easier text searching within the attached document.
  • Data Import CSV files with required fields (e.g., Date, Reference, Description, Amount, Account, Tax Rate).

Step-by-Step Conversion Workflow

Step 1 Ensure Scan Quality

Before any conversion, a clear, high-resolution scan is crucial. Blurry, skewed, or low-contrast images significantly hinder OCR accuracy, leading to errors in data extraction.

  • Set your scanner to at least 300 DPI (dots per inch) for optimal text recognition.
  • Ensure proper lighting and minimize shadows on the receipt.
  • Orient receipts correctly to avoid unnecessary rotation steps later.

Step 2 Perform Optical Character Recognition OCR

This is the most critical step for making your scanned receipts useful. OCR technology analyzes the image to identify text characters, turning an image-based PDF into a selectable, searchable document.

Utilize a reliable online OCR tool. Upload your scanned PDF, and the tool will process it to create a new, searchable PDF layer. This allows text search and selection within the document, a prerequisite for many data extraction methods. You can quickly OCR your PDF files online.

OCR Benefits for Scanned Receipts
Before OCR After OCR
Image-only PDF Searchable PDF
Cannot select text Can select and copy text
Manual data entry required Enables automated or semi-automated data extraction

Step 3 Extract Data to CSV or Excel

Once your PDF is searchable, the next step is to extract the relevant financial data. While some advanced tools offer automated receipt parsing, a common and reliable approach involves converting the searchable PDF into an editable format like Excel or manually copying data into a CSV template.

Consider converting the searchable PDF to Excel. This allows you to easily copy and paste specific fields (date, vendor, amount, tax) into a spreadsheet template compatible with QuickBooks or Xero. Many online services can convert PDF to Excel directly.

  • Open the OCR'd PDF.
  • Identify and copy key data points such as Date, Merchant, Total Amount, and any relevant line items.
  • Paste this information into your accounting software's CSV or Excel template.
  • Ensure column headers in your template exactly match the software's import requirements.

Step 4 Prepare for Import

Review the extracted data for accuracy and consistency. Accounting software is strict about data types and formats. Dates, for instance, must precisely match the expected format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY).

Double-check currency symbols, decimal places, and ensure numerical fields are formatted as numbers. Any discrepancies can cause import errors or lead to incorrect ledger entries.

Step 5 Import into QuickBooks or Xero

Navigate to the "Import Data" or "Bank Statements" section within your accounting software. The exact path may vary slightly between platforms and versions.

Upload the prepared CSV or Excel file. You will typically be prompted to map the columns from your file to the corresponding fields in QuickBooks or Xero. Confirm the import and then review the newly created transactions against your original receipts for final verification.

Best Practices for Receipt Management

To streamline this process and maintain efficient bookkeeping, integrate these practices into your routine.

  • Consistent Scanning Digitize receipts as soon as they are received to prevent loss or damage.
  • Batch Processing Group similar receipts for efficient OCR and data extraction, saving time.
  • Template Use Always use the official QuickBooks or Xero CSV templates for imports to avoid mapping errors.
  • Regular Review Periodically check imported data against original receipts for accuracy and compliance.
